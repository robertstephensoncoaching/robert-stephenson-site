About Robert

Different rooms. Different challenges. The same underlying question.

Leadership can look successful from the outside while feeling far more complicated from within.

You may be carrying greater responsibility, navigating change, stepping into a more visible role, or trying to lead people through circumstances for which there is no simple playbook. In moments like these, another leadership technique is rarely the whole answer. The deeper work is often about seeing more clearly: the situation, the system around you, the stories shaping your choices, and the person you are becoming as you lead. That's where I work.

What I believe

The person behind the leader matters.

You don't leave yourself at the office door.

The leader making the strategic decision is also a human being carrying history, ambition, relationships, assumptions, hopes, fears and stories about who they need to be. So I am interested in both. The organisational challenge. And the human being experiencing it.

My work draws from narrative coaching, the Enneagram, systems thinking, Physical Intelligence, coaching psychology and more than twenty years of working with people and groups. I am interested in the stories we inhabit. The roles we unconsciously accept. The systems we become part of. The patterns we repeat. And the possibility that sometimes a very small shift in perspective changes everything that follows.

“I'd never thought about it like that before.”

Clients often describe moments when something suddenly becomes visible. Those are the moments I work for. Because once something can be seen, we have choices about what happens next.

The journey

More than twenty years of helping people see differently.

My work has taken place in coaching conversations, leadership programmes, classrooms, executive-team sessions and rooms full of people trying to create something different together. I'm based in London, and most of that work happens in person, though I also work virtually with clients across the UK.

My professional journey has included senior leadership roles within Animas Centre for Coaching, including Centre Director, alongside my work as an executive coach, facilitator, coach educator and associate with organisations including Ivy House London, More Happi, Jenny Bird Executive Coaching, Elev-8 Performance and the Physical Intelligence Institute.

I've worked with new leaders finding their voice, experienced executives carrying significant organisational responsibility, leadership teams trying to work differently and organisations navigating growth and change. Different people. Different rooms. Different challenges.

The recurring question has been the same: what does leadership require of us now?

Over time, I have become increasingly interested in what sits beneath the visible leadership challenge: identity, story, motivation, relationships, patterns of response and the systems in which people are trying to lead. That curiosity continues to shape my coaching, facilitation and speaking.

Coaching and facilitation

Sometimes the work is with one person. Sometimes it is with the room.

In executive coaching, I offer a confidential thinking partnership in which a leader can slow things down, examine what is happening beneath the surface and make more deliberate choices.

In facilitation, I work with the whole room: the relationships, energy, participation, tensions and possibilities that exist between people.

The scale changes. The underlying work does not.

“Sometimes the work isn't with one person. It's with the room.”

In both settings, I aim to create enough trust for honesty, enough challenge for movement, and enough space for something new to become visible.

How people experience me

Warmth, challenge and real attention.

People think better when there is trust. They take meaningful risks when they feel safe enough to be honest, and they often discover more when curiosity, humanity and a little humour are present in the room.

My style is warm, direct and deeply attentive. I listen for what is being said, what is not yet being said, and what may be shaping the situation beneath the surface.

Ideas are part of the work too.

Alongside coaching and facilitation, I speak about leadership, identity, story and the inner work of becoming the leader a particular moment requires.

My emerging speaking work explores questions such as:

  • Who are you becoming as a leader?
  • What story are you leading from?
  • What becomes possible when you see differently?
  • How does the person of the leader shape the system around them?

These ideas also inform my writing and the longer body of work I am developing around The Self Who Leads.
