Narrative · Identity · Leadership transition

The story that got you here may not be the story that takes you forward.

The stories that once helped us succeed can eventually become the ones that constrain what comes next. A reflection on what happens when role, identity and possibility begin to shift.

Identity · Character · Leadership

The role we learn to play can eventually become the thing that constrains us.

Leadership often asks us to inhabit a role. The question is what happens when that role becomes too fixed, too narrow or no longer fits the person we are becoming.
