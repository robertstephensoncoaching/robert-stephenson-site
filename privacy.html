Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This is a plain-English explanation of what happens to your information if you get in touch, book a conversation, join Seeing Differently or visit this site. I've kept it short and honest rather than dense with legal boilerplate.

Who I am

This site belongs to Robert Stephenson, trading as Robert Stephenson Coaching Ltd. I'm the person responsible for your data when you interact with this website, and the person to contact if you have any questions about it.

What information I collect

I only collect what's needed to respond to you, arrange a conversation or send something you have asked to receive:

  • If you email me: I receive your name, email address, and whatever you choose to write.
  • If you book a call through Calendly: Calendly collects your name, email address, and any details you enter when booking. This is handled directly by Calendly under their own privacy policy, not stored separately by me beyond what's needed to prepare for and hold the conversation.
  • If you join Seeing Differently: MailerLite collects your email address and records your consent so I can send the monthly note you requested. You must confirm your address by email before you are subscribed, and every email includes an unsubscribe link.
  • Ordinary technical data: Like most websites, basic technical information (such as your general location and browser type) may be logged by the infrastructure that serves this site, Cloudflare. I don't use this for tracking or marketing.

What I don't do

This site doesn't currently run analytics or advertising trackers. I use MailerLite to provide the Seeing Differently signup form, manage the subscriber list and send the emails people have requested. I don't sell, rent or share subscriber information for anyone else's marketing.

Cookies

This site does not set its own advertising cookies. MailerLite's embedded signup form may use essential browser storage or cookies to operate the form and remember that it has been submitted. If you use the "Book a 20-minute chat" button, Calendly may also set cookies necessary for its booking service. Those services handle their own technical data under their respective privacy and cookie policies.

Why I use your information

To respond to your enquiry, arrange and hold a conversation, deliver agreed work, or send Seeing Differently when you have actively subscribed. For the newsletter, I rely on your consent; you can withdraw it at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any email.

How long I keep it

I keep correspondence and booking information only for as long as it's genuinely useful, for an ongoing enquiry or client relationship, and for any period required by law (for example, for accounting purposes). Newsletter details are kept while you remain subscribed and removed or anonymised when they are no longer needed.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to ask what personal information I hold about you, to have it corrected if it's wrong, or to have it deleted. If you'd like to exercise any of these, just email robert@robertstephensoncoaching.com and I'll sort it out directly.

Questions

If anything here isn't clear, or you'd like more detail on any point, email me and I'll happily explain.
