Where Might You Start?

What is happening right now?

For You

Something in your leadership needs attention.

Perhaps the role has become bigger. The stakes are higher. A decision is difficult. A relationship has become harder to navigate. You are carrying too much. Or the way you have led until now no longer feels sufficient for what comes next.

Executive Coaching

A confidential thinking partnership for senior leaders carrying responsibility, complexity or change.

For The Team

The capability is there. Something between you is getting in the way.

The conversations keep repeating. People are holding back. Conflict is being avoided or consuming too much energy. Accountability is uneven. Strong individuals are not yet operating as a strong team. Or something has changed and the team needs to find its way again.

Teams & Facilitation

Support for leadership teams that need to think, decide, relate or work differently together.

For A Different Lens

The situation may only be part of the story.

Sometimes the challenge is not only what is happening. It is the story through which we are making sense of it. The role we have learned to play. The assumptions we no longer notice. The identity we are trying to protect. Or the possibilities that have become difficult to see.

Narrative Coaching

A way of exploring the stories shaping identity, leadership, choice and what becomes possible next.

For Greater Self-Awareness

You want to understand recurring patterns more clearly.

You may be curious about how you pay attention. What drives your responses. What you repeatedly avoid. How pressure changes your behaviour. Or how difference shows up across a team.

For Organisations

You may be thinking about someone else.

Sometimes you are not looking for support for yourself. You may be thinking about:

  • a senior leader stepping into greater responsibility
  • someone navigating significant change
  • a leadership team that needs to work differently
  • a difficult conversation or team reset
  • coaching as part of wider leadership development

You do not need to design the intervention before getting in touch. We can start with what you are seeing and what you need to be different.

Not Sure?

You do not need to diagnose the support before we speak.

It may not be obvious whether the right starting point is coaching, team work, facilitation, the Enneagram or simply a conversation. That is fine. Tell me what is happening. We can begin there.

Book a Conversation → 20 minutes. No agenda. No pitch.

Just a chance to talk about what is happening, what you are looking for and whether working together might be useful. If it is not the right fit, that is a perfectly good outcome too.

The Work

Reflection, challenge and enough space to see more clearly.

My work is thoughtful, direct and curious. Sometimes we need to slow something down. Sometimes name what everyone can feel but no one is saying. Sometimes challenge an assumption. Sometimes notice the pattern underneath the immediate problem. And sometimes the most useful thing is simply to create enough space for a better choice to become visible.
