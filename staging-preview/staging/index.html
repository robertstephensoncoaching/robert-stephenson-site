What I believe

There is more richness in people than is usually visible. Sometimes even to themselves.

Leadership development isn't only about adding another skill, framework or technique. Sometimes the most important shift begins by seeing differently: noticing the story you are living inside, the role you have learned to play, the assumptions you no longer question and the possibilities that have disappeared from view. My work creates the space to bring some of that back into sight.

Robert's story

Story has been part of my work for much longer than coaching has.

I originally trained as an actor and worked in physical theatre, where character, story, relationship, movement and presence were the work.

Those same questions still fascinate me now.

  • Who are we being?
  • What role have we learned to play?
  • What happens when that role no longer serves us?
  • And what else becomes possible when we see the story differently?

Over more than 5,000 hours of coaching, facilitation and leadership development, that curiosity has grown into a practice drawing on Narrative Coaching, systemic thinking, the Enneagram, embodiment and more than two decades of working with people and organisations.
